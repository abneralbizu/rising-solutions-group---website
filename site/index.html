01Due diligence

Before a client commits, Rafael leads a full review of the property so the decision rests on facts rather than assumptions:

  • Site analysis: access, layout, utilities and how the site can be used

07Questions

Before you call.

Can I hire only Phase 1?

Yes. Each phase can be selected on its own. Many clients start with Phase 1 and decide on concept design after seeing the findings.

Is this a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment?

No. We screen for known environmental constraints in public sources, such as flood zones and mapped wetlands. A Phase I ESA is a separate study by an environmental professional, and lenders often require one.

Can the concept plan be used for permits?

No. It is schematic and not for construction. Permit drawings come from a licensed architect or engineer.

Is the pre-application meeting an approval?

No. Staff comments are informal and non-binding, which is why we document them in writing.

What do I need to provide?

The address or parcel ID, your intended use and size goals, and any deed, title report, survey, earlier reports, or known easements.
